# Your Campaign at a Glance

This is your campaign's contents page. It starts empty, and the assistant adds links as you approve new notes. For help getting started, see the [first-campaign walkthrough](../docs/getting-started.md) or [GM guide](../docs/gm-guide.md).

As your notes grow, ask the assistant for backstory, lore, storyline details, or a quick reminder during play. It should find the relevant pages, link its sources, and flag missing or uncertain information. [Campaign lookups](../docs/gm-guide.md#ask-your-campaign-a-question) do not change your files or require an approval step.
