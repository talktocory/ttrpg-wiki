# Session Log

## Session details

Date/number, participants, and source coverage.

## Confirmed events

Events established at the table; label uncertainty explicitly.

## Revealed facts

Record who learned what; do not infer every player witnessed every fact.

## Private consequences

Separate from player-visible events.

## Confirmed awards

Evidence, recipient, original unit, acquisition ID if known.

## Current stopping point

Immediate situation and unresolved hooks.

## Sources

Link relevant confirmed records and source locations; remove irrelevant headings.
