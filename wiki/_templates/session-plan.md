# Session Plan

## Session target

Date/number, expected participants, duration, tone, and immediate goals.

## Previous context

Confirmed events, unresolved hooks, character goals.

## Flexible scenes

Triggers, stakes, choices, clues, and alternatives.

## NPC and faction agendas

Knowledge and reveal gates; do not advance activity without approval.

## Secrets and reveals

Conditions required for each possible reveal.

## Resources and rewards

Conditional only until confirmed.

## Rules reminders

Configured system references and verification gaps.

## Optional assets and random tables

Maps, tokens, handouts, and prompts when useful.

## Next-session tasks

Open preparation decisions.

## Sources

Link relevant confirmed records and source locations; remove irrelevant headings.
