# Current Page Format

Maintain one current reference per entity or scene. Put current state first; session records own chronology. Omit irrelevant headings. Preserve source uncertainty and label local Draft/Unused sections on mixed pages.

NPCs: at a glance, current situation, knowledge and secrets, reusable voice, relevant scene links, optional rules/assets, concise sources.

Encounters: at a glance, current state, conditional runnable procedure, situational dialogue with reveal conditions, resolution/rewards, optional rules/assets, concise sources.

Do not duplicate scene dialogue onto NPC pages, append repeated session-update sections, create historical copies, or add empty compatibility anchors. Repair incoming links to actual headings. Preserve exact costs, limits, knowledge gates, and confirmed outcomes while reorganizing. Artwork approval does not establish fictional truth.

Keep map/token files in `assets/vtt/`. Record their links, briefs, provenance, export settings, approval, and reveal restrictions in the owning encounter, location, NPC, or player-character page's relevant asset section. Create a separate linked note in that page's wiki category only for substantial detail; do not maintain duplicate notes or a parallel VTT wiki tree.
