# Starting Points for Campaign Pages

These are examples of useful headings for different kinds of notes. You do not have to fill them out yourself or use every heading. Ask the assistant to choose the sections that help with your task, then review the proposed page before it is saved.

New material starts as Draft until its status is established. Empty headings are not a reason to invent campaign facts.

- [Session Plan](session-plan.md): prepare the next game.
- [Session Log](session-log.md): record what actually happened.
- [NPC](npc.md) and [Player Character](player-character.md): keep people and their goals current.
- [Location](location.md), [Encounter](encounter.md), and [Faction](faction.md): prepare places, scenes, and groups.
- [Item](item.md) and [Reward Ledger](reward-ledger.md): track objects and confirmed awards.
- [Player Handout](player-handout.md): write material intended for players.
- [Random Table](random-table.md): prepare options to choose or roll during play.
- [Rule Reference](rule-reference.md) and [Stat Block](stat-block.md): keep short reminders for your chosen game.
- [Asset Note](asset-note.md): useful details for maps or tokens; usually part of the owning campaign page.
- [Proposed Change](proposed-change.md): the review summary the assistant shows before editing.
- [Page Format](page-format.md): guidance for keeping NPC and encounter pages useful at the table.
