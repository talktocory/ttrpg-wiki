# Reward Ledger

## Accounting basis

Resource units, acquisition period, approved valuation policy, source coverage.

## Per-unit totals and optional budgets

Keep units separate. Unknown values are unknown, not zero. Show budget status only when a target exists.

## Pending and unresolved

Exclude promises, planned awards, and disputed amounts from confirmed totals.

## Dispositions

Transfers, spending, sales, and consumption do not automatically change gross-award totals.

## Sources

Link relevant confirmed records and source locations; remove irrelevant headings.
