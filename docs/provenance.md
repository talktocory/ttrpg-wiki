# Sharing Text and Artwork

Keep a short record of where your text and images came from, who made them, and what you are allowed to share. That source history is sometimes called provenance.

The organizing approach is adapted from [Andrej Karpathy's LLM Wiki idea file](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f). The campaign workflows and GM review/spoiler safeguards are this project's adaptation; no endorsement is implied.

The template includes original framework text, a Pillow-based token utility, and original fictional examples. It includes no rulebook extracts, commercial adventures, campaign portraits, maps, or reference tokens.

MIT covers the framework and original examples. Dependencies retain their own licenses. Imported campaign material is not relicensed by being placed here.

For added media, record creator/provider, source, permission or license, generation service if used, and approved uses beside the asset link on its owning encounter, location, NPC, or player-character page. A separate linked note in the same wiki category is optional when substantial detail warrants it. Preserve required attribution in player exports. Review redistribution rights before contributing third-party content publicly.
